SmartDeals Uploader

A personal publishing tool for the SmartDeals YouTube channel.

What this app is

SmartDeals Uploader is a small, single-operator application used by the SmartDeals team to publish its own product-review videos to its own YouTube channel. It is not a public service: it is operated by the channel owner, for the channel owner's own content.

What it does

  • Connects to the operator's own YouTube channel using Google OAuth (scopes youtube.upload and youtube), only after the operator explicitly grants access.
  • Uploads finished video files together with a title, description and tags.
  • Schedules publication by setting the video's publish date and time (published automatically by YouTube at the chosen moment).
  • Reads back basic video status and metadata after upload to confirm that each publishing job succeeded.

How it works

The tool runs locally on the operator's computer. It authenticates with the Google account that owns the YouTube channel and sends upload requests through the YouTube Data API. Videos are uploaded to the operator's own channel; nothing is posted anywhere else.

Google user data

SmartDeals Uploader accesses Google user data (YouTube account and channel information, and the ability to upload videos) only to publish videos to the channel that granted access. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. For details, see our Privacy Policy.
