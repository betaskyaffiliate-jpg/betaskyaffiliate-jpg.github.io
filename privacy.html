SmartDeals Uploader

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Applies to the application “SmartDeals Uploader”

1. Who we are

SmartDeals Uploader (“the App”, “we”) is a personal tool operated by the owner of the SmartDeals YouTube channel. It is used solely to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own YouTube channel. Contact: [email protected].

2. What Google user data the App accesses

After the operator signs in with Google and grants consent, the App may access:

  • YouTube account and channel information (scope .../auth/youtube) — channel name, channel ID and basic video status.
  • Permission to upload videos (scope .../auth/youtube.upload) — to upload video files and their metadata (title, description, tags) and to schedule publication on the operator's own channel.

The App does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

3. How we use the data

  • To upload video files chosen by the operator to the operator's own YouTube channel.
  • To set titles, descriptions, tags and scheduled publishing times for those videos.
  • To read back upload status so the operator can confirm the operation succeeded.

The data is used for no other purpose.

4. Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are used only to keep the App connected to the operator's channel.
  • Google user data is not stored on any external server controlled by the App.
  • Tokens can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the local token file can also be deleted at any time.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to any third party. Data is transmitted only to Google's YouTube Data API as required to perform the uploads described above. No data is used for advertising, profiling or any automated decision-making.

6. Limited Use disclosure

SmartDeals Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Security

The App runs locally and communicates with Google over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access is limited to the operator's own Google account and the operator's own YouTube channel.

8. YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, the operator agrees/confirms they are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
